How it works

  1. Your business signs up and gets a public link, like commentcard.app/b/harbor-lane-bakery. Print it on a receipt or a table card, or share it anywhere.
  2. Customers open the link, scroll through other people's suggestions, and leave their own — what bugged them, what they'd change, or an idea.
  3. Suggestions show up in public right away, like scrolling through comments. The business sees them too, in a private dashboard, and can mark them noted, acted on, or hide anything off-topic.

No stars. No public review pages. Just the honest stuff a business needs to hear.
